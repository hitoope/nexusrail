Systems & Safety Assurance · 30 August 2026

The standard is not the hard part

Safety assurance is routinely scoped as a set of deliverables. It is not. It is an engineering program that runs alongside the project throughout its lifecycle — and treating it otherwise is why assurance problems tend to surface late.

Let’s strengthen assurance without slowing delivery.

If you need plug-and-play assurance capacity—governance, hazard log leadership, RAMS & safety documentation, V&V and test evidence, TVRA, or independent reviews—NEXUS can integrate quickly and deliver consistently.
