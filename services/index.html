Services

Plug-and-play assurance capacity for rail and transit delivery teams.

NEXUS provides Systems Engineering support, System & Safety Assurance, Hazard Log governance, RAMS engineering, V&V evidence support, Human Factors, Security TVRA, and independent readiness reviews for LRT, Metro, HSR, and freight programs. We support public agencies, infrastructure owners, integrators, contractors, and joint ventures enable timely decisions, reduce rework, strengthen compliance, and stay acceptance-ready—from design through testing, commissioning, and operations. 

EN 50126–50129 (CENELEC) • North American delivery & review expectations (FTA / CMREA) • Audit-ready evidence • Governance & traceability 

Systems & Safety Assurance

Governance, assurance planning, compliance verification, and evidence management across the full lifecycle.

What we deliver: 

  • Systems & Safety Assurance Plans (SSA) and governance model (roles, gates, decision flow) 
  • Assurance planning aligned to delivery milestones (design, integration, testing, commissioning, entry into service) 
  • Compliance verification against applicable safety standards and Employer / project requirements 
  • Evidence management: structure, traceability rules, and readiness for reviews and acceptance 
  • Stakeholder alignment and decision support to drive closure and keep delivery moving 
  • Safety integration across disciplines and interfaces (Systems, Signalling, Track, Civil, Rolling Stock, Depot/Workshops, O&M).

Hazard Management & Analysis (Hazard Log + Human Factors)

Plug-and-play Hazard Log governance for complex rail programs. 

What we deliver: 

  • Hazard identification and analysis support (e.g., HAZID/HAZOP facilitation, IHA/OHA support, FTA inputs, FMECA integration) 
  • Hazard Log governance: data model, quality rules, ownership, workflow, statistics and status control 
  • Safety requirements definition, allocation, and traceability linked to hazards and mitigations 
  • Integration with designers, suppliers, Testing & Commissioning (T&C) and external stakeholders. 
  • Alignment with Independent Safety Assessors and external auditors 
  • Closure readiness: evidence checks, action tracking, closure workshops, and gate support 

 

Plug-and-play approach: 

We bring proven tools and workflows that accelerate hazard governance—improving consistency, traceability, and closure readiness across disciplines, suppliers, and project phases. 

RAMS Engineering

RAMS planning and analyses that support reliable performance and operational readiness. 

What we deliver: 

  • RAM and Safety documentation production aligned with program requirements, standards, and acceptance gates (plans, reports, technical memos) 
  • Safety case and technical safety justifications 
  • Safety analyses and subsystem hazard documentation as required (e.g., IHA, OHA, SSHA) and integration into program governance 
  • Safety FMECA / FMEA and related RAMS & safety analyses, integrated with hazards, mitigations, and assurance evidence 
  • Hazard Log integration: ensuring RAMS/Safety outputs feed hazard causes, mitigations, safety requirements, and closure evidence (traceability and defensibility) 
  • HAZID / HAZOP / hazard workshops support (facilitation, inputs, outputs consolidation) and conversion into actionable requirements and evidence needs 
  • Human Factors studies and report 
  • Reliability/Availability/Maintainability targets definition, allocation, and demonstration strategy (including verification approach and evidence expectations) 
  • Operational integrity and O&M readiness inputs where applicable (maintainability, maintenance concept, inspections, procedures, evidence expectations) 

Verification & Validation (V&V) and Testing Evidence Support

V&V strategy and evidence that keeps testing, commissioning, and acceptance moving. 

What we deliver: 

  • V&V strategy and verification planning aligned to lifecycle milestones and acceptance gates 
  • Evidence traceability: requirements → verification method → test evidence → closure confirmation 
  • Gap identification, prioritization, and closure tracking across stakeholders 
  • Support through commissioning and entry into service readiness 

Security – TVRA

Security risk assessment with practical mitigation planning for transit environments.

What we deliver: 

  • Cyber and physical Threat & Vulnerability Risk Assessment (TVRA) tailored to rail/transit scope 
  • Risk treatment plans and practical mitigation recommendations with ownership and closure steps 
  • Integration of security controls into assurance activities and evidence readiness 
  • Stakeholder workshops to align scope, priorities, and closure ownership 
  • Support for security readiness reviews where required 

Independent Reviews, Certification & Audit Readiness

Independent reviews, compliance alignment and objective verification support to strengthen confidence and readiness.

What we deliver: 

  • Compliance mapping and evidence alignment (e.g., CENELEC ↔ FTA/CMREA expectations) 
  • Independent reviews of RAMS/Safety documentation, assurance processes, hazard governance, and evidence quality 
  • Independent V&V support (strategy review, sampling, traceability checks, gap reporting) 
  • Safety audits / assurance audits (process and evidence sampling, findings, corrective actions, closure tracking) 
  • Review and audit preparation (internal readiness checks before external gates) 
  • Support during external reviews and stakeholder acceptance milestones, including responses and closure plans 
  • Stakeholder-facing summaries that enable clear decisions and acceptance readiness 

Additional Capabilities

Stakeholder Alignment & Governance Support

Analyze expectations, workshops, decision logs, action closure, and governance routines to align owners, contractors, suppliers, authorities and independent reviewers.

Documentation & Gate Reviews

Technical documentation, design and validation review, independent checks of evidence, ensuring completeness, traceability, and technical correctness.

Pursuit / Bid Support (Primes & JVs)

SSA/RAMS proposal inputs: scope definition, assumptions and deliverables, methodology and governance model, resourcing and effort, and support for commercial packaging—ensuring a clear, technically sound submission
